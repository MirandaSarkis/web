---
This series, made in 2022 in green ink, thinks of our bond with the landscape as a form of love: a love for the world we inhabit, for the place that holds us and that, so often, we also harm.

The drawings are built from a few elements: stones, ropes, posts, arrows, flowers. They are simple objects that enter into tension with one another. An arrow is driven into a split stone that remains standing. A rope ties, holds and at the same time immobilizes. Flowers grow, are cut, hang. Stones are piled up like apachetas, those cairns that mark the way in the mountains and that, every time I find one, make me feel accompanied.

In each image, care and wound coexist, balance and its fragility, what is beautiful and what hurts. As in any bond, nothing holds without that tension.

I chose green for its vibrancy, for its relationship with the natural world and for its capacity to cover everything. Pointillism, slow and patient, dot by dot, is my way of drawing closer to a nature from which we are ever more distant, and in which I sometimes feel at home and sometimes an intruder.
