Pie de foto: Self-portrait
---
She was born in the capital of the province of La Rioja, Argentina, in 1994; she currently lives and works in the city of Córdoba.

She studied at the Universidad Provincial de Córdoba, where she earned her degree in Visual Arts (Técnica Superior en Artes Visuales).

In 2026 she received the acquisition prize for best provincial work at the Salón Nacional de Artes Visuales de La Rioja. She was also selected for the postgraduate Specialization in Contemporary Artistic Practices at the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba (in progress, 2026–2027).

In 2025 she was selected for the Salón ¿Qué paisaje deja nuestra huella? at the Museo de Bellas Artes Evita – Palacio Ferreyra. That same year she took part again in NODO, the gallery circuit of Meridiano, with the exhibition Los encuentros impredecibles, curated by Lara Marmor at Torre Macro.

In 2024 she was selected and awarded a grant to take part in PAC, the annual artist training program coordinated by Gachi Prieto gallery, with teachers including Lorena Fernández, Ana Gallardo, Andrés Waissman and Natalia Romero, among others. In May of that year she took part in NODO, the exhibition of the gallery circuit of Meridiano, Argentina's national chamber of art galleries, at the Centro Empresarial Libertador, curated by Guadalupe Creche.

In 2023 she completed the artist training program Blasón, taught by Verónica Gómez, and was selected for the Training Program in Art, Curatorship and Contemporary Theories at the Centro de Arte of the Universidad Nacional de La Plata, coordinated by Fernando Davis; faculty: Lucía Álvarez, Fernanda Carvajal, Nicolás Cuello, Natalia Fortuny and Dani Zelko.

In 2022 she took the workshop El texto de la obra, taught by Silvia Gurfein, and began a creative writing workshop with Pablo Natale, which she completed a year later. That same year she took part in the artist residency Raíces, in Aconquija, Catamarca. She was selected and awarded a grant to take part in the first edition of the Escuela de Artes y Oficios at the Centro Cultural Kirchner, with teachers including Luis María Terán, Claudia del Río, Carlos Herrera and Dani Zelko, among others.

Between 2016 and 2018 she attended the Taller de Producción y Pensamiento (Production and Thought Workshop) led by the artist Marcos Acosta.

Her solo exhibitions include Abrí una piedra, which opened Luogo Galería's Buenos Aires space at Central Affair (2026); Hasta acá llegaste, susurra el viento, curated by Sofía Culzoni, at Ankara gallery, Colonia Caroya, Córdoba (2025); Sostener el fuego, with a text by Verónica Gómez, at Luogo Galería, Rafaela, Santa Fe (2024); No sé por cuánto tiempo caminé con los ojos cerrados, at Plegable gallery, Córdoba (2023); and Ayudame a sostener todo esto, with a text by Hugo Albrieu, at Luogo Galería, Rafaela, Santa Fe (2021). She is currently one of the artists represented by the gallery.

Her work is held in public and private collections in Argentina.
