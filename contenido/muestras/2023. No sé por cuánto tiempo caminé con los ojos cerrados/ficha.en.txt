Firma: Paloma Ludueña, translated from the Spanish
---
Hauling 30 kg of clay from La Rioja, from her father's house, to submit it to the wisdom of the hands. Trusting the earth, catching between her fingers the immensity it is made of and turning it into a portable, transportable concept: that was Miranda's strategy.

We face objects seemingly estranged from their everyday setting, even though they emerged in a studio a few blocks from the space that now houses them. To poke around in the nooks of a vessel that aims at nothing more and nothing less than containing itself, and to discover, in the meticulous reconstruction of a particular someone, a landscape that seems commonly known. There lies the fine line that reveals the human gesture in something that seems to want to evoke the memory of a vast territory.

Which territory belongs to us? Which territory do we belong to? The one that saw us born, the one that watches us grow, the one whose street names we remember most, or the one whose neighbors' names we remember most?
